# AuraFly Support

AuraFly is a cartoon and butterfly animation studio for iPhone, iPad, and Android. This page explains how to get started, export your work, and get help.

## Get started

In **Cartoon Maker**, open **Menu → Cartoon list** and tap **+ New cartoon**. You can also tap **Edit** on an example to see how it was made. Use **Cast** to add characters, **Actions** to set movement and expressions, and the scene palette to add backgrounds, props, music, and sound effects.

To give a character a voice, open its dialogue controls. Enter a line and choose an installed voice, or attach a recording. Enable mouth animation to make the character visibly speak.

## Export and share

Tap **Export** in Cartoon Maker. You can also open **Menu → Cartoon list** and tap **Export & share** next to a saved cartoon.

- **iPhone and iPad** — Choose **All scenes** or a single scene under **Export scope**. Select **MP4 video** for a movie, then tap **Save file or Share…** when it’s ready.
- **Android** — Choose **Full MP4** for the entire cartoon. To export one scene, select it in the scene strip and tap **MP4 video** in that scene’s export options. When the file is ready, tap **Save file** or **Share to apps**.

If your export is silent, preview the scene to check that the dialogue and music play, then export as MP4. On iPhone and iPad, make sure **Include voices, music and sound effects in MP4** is enabled. GIF exports are silent.

## Back up your projects

AuraFly saves projects on your device. Use **Backup + recordings** to keep an editable copy with attached audio. On iPhone and iPad, select **All scenes** for a full cartoon backup. On Android, repeat the backup for each scene you want to keep.

## Restore AuraFly Pro

Pro includes the full character library, exclusive backgrounds, additional dialogue bubble designs, and watermark-free exports. If you’ve already subscribed and Pro access is missing, open the Pro screen and tap **Restore purchases** using the store account you purchased with.
